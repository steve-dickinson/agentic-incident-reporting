# UK Environmental Legislation Reference

## Primary Legislation

### Environmental Protection Act 1990

**Scope:** Controls waste management, contaminated land, and statutory nuisances

**Key Provisions:**
- Duty of care for waste handling
- Powers to remedy contaminated land
- Control of emissions and pollutants
- Enforcement and penalties for violations

**Relevant to:**
- Illegal waste dumping
- Fly-tipping incidents
- Industrial emissions
- Land contamination

### Water Resources Act 1991

**Scope:** Protection of water resources and regulation of water abstractions

**Key Provisions:**
- Makes it an offense to pollute controlled waters
- Regulations for water abstraction
- Powers for Environment Agency enforcement
- Anti-pollution works and operations

**Relevant to:**
- Water pollution incidents
- Discharge consents
- Abstraction licensing
- Flood defense

### Wildlife and Countryside Act 1981

**Scope:** Conservation of wild animals and plants

**Key Provisions:**
- Protection of endangered species
- Special protection for SSSIs
- Penalties for habitat destruction
- Licensing for protected species management

**Relevant to:**
- Wildlife harm incidents
- Habitat destruction
- Protected site damage
- Illegal persecution of wildlife

### Clean Air Act 1993

**Scope:** Control of air pollution from domestic and industrial sources

**Key Provisions:**
- Smoke control areas
- Chimney height regulations
- Grit and dust emissions
- Enforcement powers

**Relevant to:**
- Air pollution incidents
- Industrial emissions
- Bonfire complaints
- Smoke nuisance

## EU-Retained Legislation

### Water Framework Directive (2000/60/EC)

**Status:** Retained in UK law post-Brexit with amendments

**Objectives:**
- Achieve good ecological status for water bodies
- Prevent deterioration of water quality
- Promote sustainable water use

**Classification Framework:**
- High status
- Good status
- Moderate status
- Poor status
- Bad status

### Habitats Directive (92/43/EEC)

**Status:** Retained as Conservation of Habitats and Species Regulations 2017

**Protections:**
- Special Areas of Conservation (SACs)
- Protection of European Protected Species
- Habitat assessment requirements
- Strict protection measures

## Regulatory Framework

### Environment Agency Powers

**Investigation:**
- Right to enter premises
- Power to take samples
- Requirement for information
- Use of monitoring equipment

**Enforcement:**
- Warning letters
- Enforcement notices
- Stop notices
- Suspension/revocation of permits
- Prosecution

**Penalties:**
- Unlimited fines for serious offenses
- Imprisonment up to 5 years for worst cases
- Fixed penalty notices (£50-£400)
- Costs recovery for cleanup

### Local Authority Powers

**Environmental Health:**
- Statutory nuisance investigations
- Abatement notices
- Emergency prohibition
- Works in default

**Waste Enforcement:**
- Fly-tipping investigations
- Fixed penalty notices (up to £400)
- Removal of waste
- Vehicle seizure

## Protected Site Designations

### Special Areas of Conservation (SAC)

Highest level of protection for habitats and species of European importance.

**Examples:**
- Chilterns Beechwoods
- Peak District Dales
- The Wash

**Incident Response:**
- All incidents must be reported to Natural England
- Ecological assessment required
- Restoration plan mandatory
- Potential for significant penalties

### Sites of Special Scientific Interest (SSSI)

Protected areas designated for wildlife, geology, or landform features.

**Consent Requirements:**
- Permission needed for potentially damaging operations
- Notification to Natural England mandatory
- Management agreements with landowners

**Incident Impact:**
- Damage can result in restoration notices
- Fines up to £20,000 or imprisonment
- Unlimited fines on conviction

### National Nature Reserves (NNR)

Protected areas managed for conservation, research, and public access.

**Protection:**
- Bylaws prohibit damaging activities
- Enhanced monitoring
- Active management programs

## Incident Severity Matrix

| Impact | Protected Site | Non-Protected | Public Health Risk |
|--------|---------------|---------------|-------------------|
| **Critical** | SAC/SSSI major damage | Major pollution event | Immediate risk to life |
| **High** | SSSI minor damage | Significant pollution | Potential health impact |
| **Medium** | Local Wildlife Site | Localized pollution | Minor nuisance |
| **Low** | No site affected | Minimal impact | No health concern |

## Notification Requirements

**Immediate (within 1 hour):**
- Major pollution incidents
- Public health emergencies
- Hazardous substance releases
- SAC/SSSI critical incidents

**Urgent (within 24 hours):**
- High priority pollution
- SSSI damage
- Protected species harm
- Significant fly-tipping

**Standard (within 5 days):**
- Medium priority incidents
- Minor pollution
- Routine enforcement matters
